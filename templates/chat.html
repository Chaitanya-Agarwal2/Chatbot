Problem statement

Students at a college often need quick answers to routine questions such as attendance, fee dues, exam rules, library fines, hostel timings and mess menus. Right now they have to visit several offices, wait in queues, or search through notice boards and PDFs. Office staff answer the same questions again and again, and students sometimes miss deadlines or pay fines because the information is hard to find.

This project is a student helpdesk chatbot for a fictional college, Aravali Institute of Technology. After signing in with a roll number and password, a student can ask questions in plain language and get instant answers based on their own records and the college's rules. It also flags things that need attention, such as low attendance or an overdue library book.

Objective: reduce the time and effort students spend finding routine college information.
Scope: a prototype using fictional data for one demo student.
Tools: Python (Flask), HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and an AI language model via the Groq API.
